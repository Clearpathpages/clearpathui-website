FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, so pricing depends on what you need. Send a quick quote request and you'll get a clear price before any work starts.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. ClearpathUI is strictly remote. We'll work together by phone, email and video call, wherever you're located.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. Website upgrades are one of the main services. Share your current site in the quote form and I'll look at what can be improved.

What is a Google Business Profile?

It's the free listing that shows your business on Google Search and Maps, with your hours, phone number, website and reviews. I can create a new one or tidy up the one you have.

What does "basic SEO" include?

The essentials that help search engines understand your site: page titles and descriptions, clear headings, image descriptions, and Google Search Console setup.
